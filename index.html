Email CV LinkedIn

Research Focus and Contributions

Structured model memory
Model embedding spaces that organize specialized models by their encoded knowledge, supporting efficient retrieval, reuse, merging, and continual updating.
Efficient adaptation
Sparse and filter-subspace methods that adapt less than 1% of model parameters across convolutional networks, transformers, and generative models.
Compositional learning
Structured representations that expose relations among models and enable learned concepts, skills, and task adaptations to be selectively combined.
