Research

Methods for adaptable and reliable learning.

My work studies the structure inside learned models and uses that structure to compare, customize, and transfer their knowledge.

01

Representation and model structure

Neural networks through their learned filters and subspaces, with emphasis on similarity, sparse structure, and efficient tuning.

02

Generative and unsupervised learning

Diffusion model customization and optimal transport methods for controllable learning from diverse examples.

03

Learning across change

Continual and federated learning under catastrophic forgetting, heterogeneous data, and adversarial collaboration.
